Founders

Meet the four people behind Intent United

The officers leading our mission to expand educational equity and student access.

President

Rahil Shah

Rahil is dedicated to driving the core mission of Intent United forward by expanding educational equity and student access. With a strong background in community organization and strategic planning, he focuses on building sustainable support systems that empower students to reach their full potential.

Vice President

Anthony Zeng

Anthony brings sharp organizational leadership and a passion for mentorship to the team. His work centers on fostering collaborative partnerships, streamlining operations, and ensuring that every student involved with Intent United receives the guidance they need to succeed.

Head of Outreach

Yisheng Yao

Yisheng leads community engagement and education initiatives, bridging the gap between Intent United and the students we serve. Through thoughtful design, clear communication, and active outreach, Yisheng helps make learning resources welcoming and accessible to all.

Head of Technology

Kaan Malaxa

Kaan spearheads the technical strategy and digital infrastructure behind Intent United. By combining technical expertise with a commitment to nonprofit growth, Kaan builds reliable tools that help scale the organization's reach and impact.
