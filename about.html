About Intent United

Helping children in hard circumstances learn with confidence and care

Intent United works to support young children in underserved parts of India by combining safe AI learning tools with strong local mentorship and community partnerships.

Our mission

We aim to make learning more accessible for children facing poverty, school disruption, language barriers, and limited educational support.

Our approach

We use AI as a support layer, not a replacement for people, so every tool remains practical, understandable, and grounded in local relationships.

What makes us different

Simple systems built around real communities

Age appropriate AI

Every learning experience is designed to stay clear, supportive, and easy for young children to use.

Local partnership

We work with educators, volunteers, and community centers that understand the children they serve.

Practical delivery

Our tools are designed for shared devices, low stress use, and environments where resources may be limited.
